True success is not simply a matter of accolades.

My philosophy is that actualizing wellbeing and potential is an art form—an ongoing process of creative self-discovery, shaped by the interplay of our internal world and environment.

“My work focuses not simply on developing skills, but on understanding the person behind the performance.”

I earned my doctorate in clinical psychology from Nova Southeastern University in Fort Lauderdale, Florida, with a concentration in psychodynamic psychotherapy, trauma, personality disorders, and relationship difficulties.

My clinical experience has included working with patients across inpatient and outpatient settings, including private practice, community mental health centers, and training at Jackson Health System/University of Miami Miller School of Medicine.

Before my career in psychology, I competed as an athlete at the international and collegiate levels, which continues to inform my work. Having lived across the United States and Europe, including Miami, New York City, Austria, and Portugal, I bring a global and multicultural perspective.

Today, I apply my experience both clinically and beyond the therapy room. My work with high-performing individuals focuses not simply on developing skills, but on understanding the person behind the performance.

I help clients develop greater self-awareness, capitalize on their strengths, address patterns that may limit their effectiveness, and build more sustainable ways of performing and leading.

I have worked with high-performing and high-profile individuals worldwide, including professional athletes in the NFL and NBA, C-suite executives, academics, medical professionals, entrepreneurs, and creatives.
