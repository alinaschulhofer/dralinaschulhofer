Consulting

Consulting, education, and leadership advisory — for teams and organizations seeking sustainable excellence and wellbeing.

Alongside my clinical practice, I'm the founder of Architecture of Excellence™, offering organizational consulting, education, leadership advisory, and executive coaching for high-performing individuals, teams & organizations worldwide.

Architecture of Excellence™ is based on a proprietary framework that fuses psychoanalytic theory with high-performance strategy, informed by my clinical background, my work with high performers, and my own experience as a former elite athlete.

The services offered through Architecture of Excellence™ are distinct from clinical therapy, being focused on performance, leadership, and organizational excellence rather than the treatment of mental health conditions.
