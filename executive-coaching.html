Executive Coaching

Private, 1:1 coaching for those operating at the highest levels of their field — executives, founders, leaders, professional athletes, and others committed to closing the gap between where they are and what they're truly capable of.

What to ExpectA highly individualized, retainer-based engagement built around your goals.

Each engagement is highly individualized. We begin with an in-depth conversation to understand your current context, your goals, and the specific obstacles shaping your performance, wellbeing, relationships, and leadership.

From there, the work unfolds according to your unique needs. Engagements are retainer-based and structured over a minimum of three months, allowing for the depth, continuity, and trust that meaningful transformation requires.

Areas of FocusLeadership presence, decision-making, and the patterns that shape performance.
  • Identification of patterns limiting leadership and performance growth
  • Relational challenges and emotional intelligence
  • Leadership presence, clarity, and decision-making
  • Navigating pressure, burnout, and complex organizational demands
  • Clarification of vision, values, and legacy
  • Ongoing strategic support and accountability
Who This Is ForExecutives, founders, professional athletes, and other leaders committed to sustainable excellence.

Designed for those who demand excellence — executives, founders, professional athletes, and other high performers committed to closing the gap between where they are and their highest potential.

All individual services are delivered by Dr. Schulhofer directly. She accepts a limited number of 1:1 clients at any given time, to give each individual the highest quality of attention, depth, and continuity.

All coaching engagements are available internationally and are structured as private retainers. Investment details are discussed during the initial consultation.
