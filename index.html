Welcome!

I'm a licensed psychologist in Florida and New York. I specialize in working with high-performers and high-profile individuals — including executives, entrepreneurs, creatives, founders, and professional athletes — who often navigate complex inner lives alongside demanding professional responsibilities.

Behind outward success, many carry internal struggles they rarely feel permitted to voice: pressure, self-doubt, relationship difficulties, or a sense of disconnection from themselves. I offer a confidential space to explore what lies beneath the surface.

My approach goes beyond symptom management, supporting long-lasting transformation and overall wellbeing. Within my clinical practice, my areas of expertise include trauma, relationship difficulties, and personality.

I work well with all kinds of individuals, couples, and families who are willing to explore their inner world and ready to take responsibility for their growth. My approach is best suited for those who are willing to be honest with themselves and explore their patterns, even if it's uncomfortable at times.

My Philosophy

My approach to therapy is relational psychoanalytic and founded on the premise that healing occurs within the context of a safe and consistent relationship that allows for the exploration and expression of your true self.

I believe that the therapeutic process offers the opportunity to become more truthful with ourselves and others, and to gain a better understanding of the repetitive thoughts, feelings, and patterns that prevent us from living a healthier, more successful and fulfilled life.

While my practice has emphasized trauma, relationship issues, and personal growth, I work with individuals, couples, and families navigating a range of concerns. These include: borderline and narcissistic personality styles, identity issues, anxiety, depression, life transitions, and neurodiverse couples.

I take a holistic and individualized approach, recognizing that each person is shaped by their unique history and experiences. Yet, my way of working remains the same regardless of the presenting problem: insight-oriented and committed to creating long-lasting transformation.
