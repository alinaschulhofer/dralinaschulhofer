My Philosophy

My approach is relational psychoanalytic in nature, a perspective that informs all aspects of my work. Whether in therapy, coaching, or consulting, I believe meaningful growth begins with a deeper understanding of ourselves: the experiences that have shaped us, our patterns, the ways we relate to ourselves and others, and the unconscious forces that influence how we see and navigate the world.

Contrary to models focused on deficiencies and diagnosis of “mental disorders,” I take a holistic perspective that sees and treats the person as an interconnected being—including mind, body, spirit, and the broader systems in which we live and work. Understanding what gets in our way is important, but so is recognizing the strengths, possibilities, and meaning within our stories.

While we will work to uncover the patterns that contribute to your challenges, my goal is also to help you discover the beauty in your story and remove the obstacles that prevent you from moving forward with greater purpose, meaning, and fulfillment.

Clinical Background

I'm a Florida + New York licensed psychologist, executive coach, and organizational consultant. I earned my doctorate in clinical psychology from Nova Southeastern University in Fort Lauderdale, Florida, with a concentration in psychodynamic psychotherapy and specialized training in trauma and dissociation, personality disorders, and relationship difficulties.

My clinical experience has included working with patients across inpatient and outpatient settings, including private practice, community mental health centers, and training at Jackson Health System/University of Miami Miller School of Medicine. This background has given me a depth of understanding of personality, relationships, emotional functioning, and the often-unconscious patterns that shape how people think, behave, lead, and relate to others.

Individual TherapyDesigned to address your unique story and specific goals.

Designed to address your unique story and specific goals, we will explore the experiences that have shaped you, identify the cycles you feel stuck in, and help you gain a deeper understanding of yourself.

For your initial session, we will meet for 60-minutes to establish an understanding of who you are, your past and present, current concerns, and what you hope to gain from therapy. This initial meeting is also an opportunity to determine goodness of fit between us.

Therapy sessions are typically held on a weekly basis, though frequency will be determined collaboratively based on your individual needs and desires. For various reasons, some may benefit from deeper, more intensive treatment where sessions take place multiple times per week.

I generally do not recommend meeting less than once per week, as infrequent sessions make it more difficult to facilitate change and experience notable improvements. While standard therapy sessions are 50-minutes in length, extended sessions may be preferable and can be arranged on a case-by-case basis.

Couples & Family TherapyIntended to address your current issues while also promoting long-term relational growth and greater intimacy.

Understanding that every relationship comes with its unique challenges, my approach focuses on your current issues while also promoting long-term relational growth and greater intimacy.

We will begin with a comprehensive initial evaluation, designed to help me understand your relationship dynamic and determine the most effective path forward. This process consists of:

  • One 60-minute joint session, followed by
  • Two individual 60-minute sessions

These sessions ideally take place within the same week so that we can identify patterns, clarify goals, and establish a therapeutic direction.

Following the initial evaluation, couples typically meet with me weekly for 50-minute sessions. Extended sessions (90-minutes) or intensives may also be arranged for those seeking more support or accelerated progress.

I draw from a variety of approaches, including psychodynamic therapy, systems and attachment theory, and emotionally-focused therapy (EFT), to help couples and families understand their patterns, communicate more effectively, and build a stronger, more connected relationship.

Intensive SessionsA focused, transformative alternative to weekly sessions — for depth, momentum, or a demanding schedule.

Therapy intensives offer a more focused and transformative experience than standard weekly sessions. Designed for individuals and couples seeking greater depth or momentum, intensives allow us to work for extended periods in a contained way.

An intensive may be helpful if you are experiencing:

  • A significant life transition
  • An interpersonal crisis
  • Trauma that feels difficult to approach in shorter sessions
  • Feeling stuck in ongoing patterns
  • A desire for accelerated personal or relational growth

Intensives can take place as:

  • Extended 90-minute or 2-hour sessions
  • Full-day (4–5 hour) intensives with breaks

Intensives are available for both individuals and couples and are tailored to your goals. They can be scheduled as standalone experiences or integrated into ongoing therapy.

My Philosophy

Derived from the two ancient Greek words psyche (“spirit” or “soul”) and therapeia (“healing”), psychotherapy is literally defined as “healing of the soul.” Trauma, loss, and other difficult experiences can cause us to lose touch with our innermost selves, leading to suffering, feelings of isolation and disconnection. Depression, anxiety, loneliness, relationship problems, and lack of purpose often result from difficulty knowing and understanding ourselves and the dynamics of our unconscious.

My approach to therapy is relational psychoanalytic and founded on the premise that healing occurs within the context of a safe and consistent relationship that allows for the exploration and expression of your true self. The therapeutic process offers the opportunity to become more truthful with ourselves and others, and to gain a better understanding of the repetitive thoughts, feelings, and patterns that prevent us from living a healthier and more fulfilled life.

My hope is to help facilitate the experience of creative self-discovery, to help you be and become more fully alive, and more fully yourself. I believe that you have innate knowledge that makes you your own greatest teacher, and combine my clinical training with my intuitive abilities to help you reconnect with the most authentic parts of you that have become lost or buried over time.
